   We presented contact-field registration followed by an-
chored capture. Physical direction cues constrain field slopes
and improve the tested target estimates. A bounded reference
optimization accommodates residual error with a discrete
storage inequality. Across ten exploration groups, PCF+RAC
completes 129/130 target-frame boundary captures, versus
102/130 for registered compliance and 76/130 for capture
at the prior, and 9/10 camera-case captures. The retained
failure limits the demonstrated range. Hardware registration
error remains limiting, and a unique benefit of the anchor
directions is not established. Prospective hardware capture
and range tests remain necessary. The theory concerns the
reference subsystem, not whole-robot stability or guaranteed
docking completion.
