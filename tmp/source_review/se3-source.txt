  Recently, a six-DoF impedance (admittance) controller has been
  proposed that uses a dual quaternion logarithm map [15], [16]. It
  resolves the topological obstruction problem in the orientation
  part by introducing a quaternion logarithmic variable, achieved
  using quaternion algebra. Still, the dual quaternion is also a
  nonminimal representation (eight parameters) for six-DoF rigid
  body motion. It is due to the lack of minimal representations that
  do not suffer from the representation singularity.
